## What's new

- PDF to DOCX with extracted text and tables.
- Video to animated GIF and animated WebP to GIF.
- XLSX/XLSM to legacy XLS.
- ZIP to PDF by merging contained images, with zip-slip protection.
- PPT/WPS to PNG/JPG page images in a ZIP.
- PDF split and password-based decryption actions.
- PDF-to-XLSX quality gate: low-confidence scanned pages fail with a clear bilingual reason instead of returning an unusable workbook.
